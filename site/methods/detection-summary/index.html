    Basic detection summary (O − E) and QC rows¶

    Implements giap.context.summary (basic_rows, qc_rows, detection_summary) and giap.io.summary_tsv.write_detection_summary (GIAP_SPEC.md §7.3.5(a), §8.2, §9.3; issue M4-16). Statistician sign-off: see the end of this note (approved after the review fixes, 2026-10-15).

    O − E against Σ p_detect¶

    For a group G of eligible genes (detection_status != partial_excluded; single-peptide genes are eligible and undetected in the primary analysis), O = Σ d_g and E = Σ p_g with the cross-fitted p_detect of M4-13, so E[O − E] = 0 when the detectability model is calibrated (§8.2: never against raw counts). Reported: n_genes, n_detected, pct_detected (0–100), expected, o_minus_e, o_over_e (NA when E = 0) and n_tu; n = n_genes on every row.

    TU-block CI¶

    O − E = Σ_TU s_t with s_t = Σ_{g ∈ G ∩ t} (d_g − p_g): only the group's genes inside a drawn TU count. The 95 % percentile CI comes from tu_block_bootstrap (docs/methods/bootstrap.md) over the group's TU sums, B = stats.bootstrap (1000). Groups are processed in sorted (group_type, group) order from one for_step("context_stats", seed) generator, so the draws for a group depend only on the TU counts of the groups before it, not on gene labels outside it. A group carries the flag ci_few_units (on the o_minus_e row only) when it has fewer than 10 TUs or when min(E, n_genes − E) < 10 (lead decision 4); a single-TU group has a degenerate CI equal to the estimate. Calibration: tests/calibration/test_detection_summary_calibration.py (coverage of 0 under a TU-comonotone null, where gene-level resampling under-covers).

    What the CI does and does not cover: it is the sampling variability of Σ (d − p) over the group's TUs given p_detect; the estimation error of the cross-fitted model is not propagated. For a group that is (nearly) the whole eligible set (the main chromosome, cog_category none without function annotation) Σ (d − p̂) ≈ 0 by construction (the logistic intercept fits calibration in the large, up to cross-fitting noise), so its O − E is a sanity check, not a finding.

    Gate cost (ADR-0015): at the measured rate 0.046 the fast gate (R = 100, ≤ 9 misses) fails a block with probability 0.017 and the full gate (R = 1000, ≤ 63) 0.006; two-block false alarms 3 × 10⁻⁴ and 3 × 10⁻⁵ per night, negligible against the ≈ 0.04 nightly budget. Power against gene-level resampling (0.166): 0.96 (fast), 1.0 (full).

    The TU count alone does not govern accuracy. The percentile CI of a sum of residuals d − p fails when few TUs carry the minority outcome (few detections when p is small, few non-detections when p is near 1): with no detection in the group every TU sum is negative and the CI excludes 0 whatever the TU count. sORF and pseudogene groups (low p) and small COG letters are exactly this case. A CI should be read as 95 % only when ≥ 10 TUs and ≥ 10 expected minority events (min(E, n_genes − E) ≥ 10); below, coverage is 0.63–0.95 (table). CIs from 10–19 TUs cover ≈ 0.91 (c(n) of bootstrap.md), so ci_few_units at < 10 is a lenient threshold.

    Groups¶

    replicon (each contig), feature_class (cds, sorf, pseudogene, and hypothetical = any feature type with hypothetical = true), cog_category: a gene is counted once in every letter of its cog_category (COG convention; KT counts in K and in T), genes without a category in none. Groups without eligible genes are omitted.

    QC rows (section = qc, run/all)¶

    • id_mixups (§7.5.1: same-database ALT sites without start_codon/stop_codon): NA with flag requires_variants until the variant module (M7) exists (lead decision 6).
    • engine_protein_mismatch_rate: distinct C11 peptides flagged engine_protein_mismatch over distinct placed (C11) peptides; n = that denominator; NA without C11 rows. A QC proxy reported under its own name, not id_mixups.
    • genome_inconsistent_rate (§7.5.1): among distinct C9 target, non-contaminant peptides with search_db = other, the share without an exact C11 placement (absent, or tolerant only); n = that denominator; NA without such peptides (e.g. all search_db = same) (lead decision 5). §7.5.1 defines it under --genome-relation same; under related tolerant placements are expected and the rate is not a mismatch signal (it is still computed; read it accordingly).
    • contaminant_peptides, contaminant_proteins, unmapped_peptides, unmapped_proteins: C13 rows by label and kind; NA without C13.

    Table¶

    Sections in order tier, basic, detectability, qc, then group_type, group, metric. Parts without n/flags (M4-12, M4-13 rows) get NA / empty. Floats are written with round-trip precision (shortest repr) rather than %.6g, so CIs read back exactly (writer tests require 1e-9).

    The E rule counts expected detections in genes; under within-TU dependence the effective count is the number of TUs that carry a detection (≈ E / TU size when detections are comonotone). Unflagged groups (≥ 10 TUs and min(E, n − E) ≥ 10) at the boundary:

    For realistic operon sizes (2–5 genes) the unflagged boundary covers 0.88–0.93, the same order as the accepted 10–19-TU regime (≈ 0.91), and coverage recovers once E is a few multiples of 10 (0.059 at E = 30). Only long, uniformly low-p TUs (20 × 10 genes) fall far below; that is not a realistic group. Recommendation (non-blocking, backlog): flag in TU units as well, e.g. fewer than 10 TUs with an observed detection (or non-detection) in the group.
