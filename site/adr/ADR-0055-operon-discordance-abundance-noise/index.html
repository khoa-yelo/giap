    ADR-0055: Operon discordance under abundance-dependent Δ noise (attack D-1)¶

    • Status: accepted-by-khoa (2026-10-24, in session: "1. Go with (a)"): option (a), σ as a function of mean abundance, with attack D-1b gated before the claim
    • Date: 2026-10-24
    • Spec sections: §7.4.2 (within-operon distribution), §7.4.3 (discordance test), §8.1 row "Operon discordance", §8.4 suite discordance, §8.5, §13 gate M6
    • Needs Khoa: yes. Either the method changes (§7.4.2) or the claim is narrowed. A failing attack blocks the gate, and no threshold is widened (R3).

    Context¶

    §7.4.2 fits one Student-t (μ, σ, ν) to Δ over all usable OP pairs, and §7.4.3 tests every both-detected OP pair against it. Real proteomics has more noise at low abundance, so the spread of Δ shrinks as the mean abundance of the pair grows. The PR #147 statistician review owed attack D-1 to M6-09. In D-1, Δ − μ is scaled by exp(−0.5 z), with z the standardised mean log2 abundance of the pair, on operon_null(1800). It is implemented in _noise_by_abundance in tests/calibration/attacks/test_attack_operons.py.

    M6-09 measured it through the merged step (Slurm 1884412 at 90a0e709, rerun as Slurm 1884493 at 24267fd3 with identical pooled numbers; R = 1,000 genomes). The bounds are the §8.4 level bounds; the quartile bound treats the pairs as independent.

    check α = 0.01 0.05 0.10
    pooled level (200 random tested pairs per genome) 0.0076 (≤ 0.0104) 0.0521 (≤ 0.0510) 0.1064 (≤ 0.1013)
    lowest-abundance quartile, cut on the planted z (123,766 pairs) 0.0273 (≤ 0.0106) 0.157 (≤ 0.0512) 0.277 (≤ 0.1017)
    same data, no D-1 (the gate null) 0.0098 0.0500 0.0998
    • The pooled level fails at α = 0.05 and 0.10, 4.2 % and 6.4 % above nominal, and the failure is confirmed in both blocks (ADR-0015).
    • The lowest quartile runs at about 3× nominal, as the PR #147 oracle found (2.4–3×).
    • The fast tier fails the same way: R = 100, pooled 0.110 against 0.104 at α = 0.10.
    • Mechanism: one σ for all pairs is too narrow for noisy low-abundance pairs and too wide for precise high-abundance pairs. Null calls therefore land on low-abundance pairs. BH over the family does not repair the per-pair error.

    Both checks now form one strict xfail naming this ADR (test_attack_d1_level). This follows the ADR-0049 pattern for the M7 gate. The M6 discordance claim is not met until this ADR is decided.

    Options¶

    (a) Model σ as a function of mean abundance (method change, §7.4.2). - The model is σ_i = exp(log σ₀ + γ z_i), with z_i the standardised mean log2 abundance of pair i. Location and ν stay as now. The fit is maximum likelihood over (μ, log σ₀, γ, ν), using the same Nelder–Mead routine and ν bounds as fit_student_t. - The p-value is 2 · sf_t(|Δ − μ̂| / σ̂_i; ν̂). - γ = 0 recovers the current method. A likelihood-ratio test of γ = 0 can be reported, but it is not used to choose between the models (no selection step). - Statistician check (a scratch oracle fit outside the code, not reproducible from the repo, R = 150 genomes; the implementer PR must re-measure it through D-1 and D-1b at the gate's R):

    data pooled level, α = 0.01 / 0.05 / 0.10 lowest quartile γ̂
    D-1 data 0.0103 / 0.0510 / 0.1004 0.0085 / 0.047 / 0.095 mean −0.508 (true −0.5)
    the null (no D-1) 0.0100 / 0.0507 / 0.1008 0.011 / 0.054 / 0.105 mean −0.004

    So it restores the level in the lowest quartile and costs nothing measurable under the null. - Caveat: the log-linear σ is the shape D-1 plants, so this is a correctly specified check. A misspecified shape (a step or a curve) has to be added as attack D-1b before the gate is claimed. - Effort: about 60 lines of implementation: - a fit_student_t_scaled(delta, z) in giap.stats.tdist; - wiring in giap.operons.discordance, plus effect in σ̂_i units, optional; - the methods note, about 40 lines; - about 150 test lines (unit tests, the γ = 0 reduction, D-1 and D-1b).

    That is one implementer PR plus a statistician review, roughly one feature-loop cycle. The z of a pair uses only the two observed abundances, so the step needs no new inputs. - With fewer than 20 usable pairs, or with a non-converged fit, the current too_few_op_pairs rule still applies.

    (b) Narrow the claim. - Discordance calls are claimed only under Δ noise that does not depend on abundance. - operons.tsv and operon_flags.tsv carry a flag such as homoscedastic_assumption, and the report says the calls are anti-conservative for low-abundance pairs when noise grows at low abundance. - The D-1 tests stay strict xfails, and the gate is claimed as narrowed. - Effort is small (a flag, a sentence, the methods note), but the per-pair claim is then false exactly where proteomics noise is largest, and nothing in a run can tell the user whether the assumption holds.

    (c) Stratify the fit by abundance quartile (a variant of (a)). - Fit one t per quartile of pair-mean abundance, each with ≥ 20 pairs, otherwise falling back to the pooled fit, and test each pair against the fit of its own stratum. - No functional form is needed, but each fit uses about a quarter of the pairs: about 115 at 1,800 genes, close to the n ≈ 50 regime in which ν̂ sits on a bound (docs/methods/operons.md, PR #147 table). - Strata boundaries also create discontinuities: a pair on either side of a boundary gets a different null.

    Recommendation¶

    (a), with (b)'s label as the interim until (a) merges: - (a) is the only option that restores the per-pair level where the failure is, at no measurable cost under the null. - It reduces exactly to the current method when γ̂ = 0. - It is also the remedy the PR #147 review listed: "σ modelled as a function of mean abundance".

    Gate D-1b (a non-log-linear σ, for example a step at the median abundance) together with D-1 at the same R before the M6 discordance claim is made.

    Decision¶

    Pending Khoa.

    Consequences¶

    • Until the decision: the M6 gate (simulation half) is not met for discordance. The missed-operon half and §7.4.7 criteria 1–3 on the gate null pass (docs/methods/operons.md, "The M6 gate, simulation half").
    • With (a): an implementer issue on giap.stats.tdist and giap.operons.discordance. The D-1 xfails become plain gated tests (an XPASS of a strict xfail fails the run, which forces the swap), and D-1b is added. M6-05H's P(miss) takes its ratio sample from the scaled fit: standardised residuals mapped back at the partner's z.
    • With (b): the flag goes into the §9.3 enum (an interface change, ADR-0036 style), the report text changes, and the xfails stay with the narrowed claim recorded in the spec sign-off.

    Amendment 1: the claim covers smooth, monotone σ(abundance) (accepted-by-khoa, 2026-10-24)¶

    • Status: accepted-by-khoa (2026-10-24, in session: "Yes and yes", answering "Do you accept the amendment (option 1)?")
    • Measured (statistician, R = 1,000, M6-13 spec): log-linear σ(abundance) passes D-1 (pooled 0.0505 at α 0.05; lowest quartile 0.0478), D-1b-floor and D-1c. It does not pass D-1b-step (a step change in noise at the median abundance): pooled 0.0518 against a bound of 0.0510; Q2 1.8×. No flexible model tried (quadratic, cubic, spline, variance floor) passed it.
    • Decision: the M6 discordance claim is "pooled level under smooth, monotone σ(abundance)". Per-quartile rates are recorded (up to 1.7×, against about 3× under the constant scale). D-1b-step stays a strict xfail naming this amendment (full tier; non-strict in the fast tier, where it cannot be detected). No bound, alpha or R changes (R3). A variance-floor σ model goes to the backlog (M6-13 follow-up).
