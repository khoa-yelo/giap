    ADR-0049: M7 variant-call precision under de novo errors (G1, A6a, A6b, A6c)¶

    • Status: accepted-by-khoa (2026-10-23)
    • Date: 2026-10-23
    • Spec sections: §7.5 (call rule, flags), §8.4 (f1_min.variants_high 0.90), §8.5 attack 6, §13 gate M7
    • Needs Khoa: yes. The fix changes the variant call rule, and the gate threshold must not be widened (R3).

    Context¶

    The M7-13a test-author's reference run (Slurm 1872235) shows the M7 variants gate failing on the current call rule: - G1: F1 is 0.838 at 100k with R = 20 (P 0.776, Rc 0.910), and 0.852 at 1M over 10 replicates. Both are below f1_min 0.90, and precision is the cause. The non-mass_equivalent subset has P 0.951 and Rc 0.675. - A6a: 0–3 isobaric non-swap ALTs per replicate are not flagged mass_equivalent. An N-terminal block read such as AG→Q aligns as the substitution G>Q. - A6b: at random adjacent swaps the share of replicates with a high call is 0.95. Two overlapping true peptides that carry the same swap give two distinct peptides. - A6c: systematic swaps are expected to fail (M7-13 risk 1). - Rows outside G1 (statistician, #255). Besides the 669 gated substitution predictions, GIAP emits 277 high rows that are not substitutions (block 218, swap 49, frameshift 10). §7.5.6 speaks of "high ALT calls", so if these are false, precision over all high rows is 0.549 (F1 0.685). Whether G1 covers them (M7-13 Q2 scopes G1 to substitutions) is part of the choice.

    Options (for Khoa)¶

    1. Call rule: mass_equivalent substitutions and swap-type ALTs never reach high without support from a peptide that is not mass-equivalent (or a reference spectrum).
    2. Alignment: treat N-terminal two-residue block reads as blocks, and flag them mass_equivalent.
    3. Distinct-peptide support: require that the distinct supporting peptides disagree at more than the swapped pair (for example, different cleavage ends).
    4. Gate scope: gate G1 on the non-mass_equivalent set. This changes what §8.4 measures. On current evidence it also fails: F1 0.790 (P 0.951, Rc 0.675). Option 1 is likely to cost recall the same way, since it removes the same calls from high.

    Decision (Khoa, 2026-10-23)¶

    1. Mass-equivalent calls stay in high, flagged. A substitution, block or swap whose ALT is mass-equivalent to the reference keeps its tier if it meets the current call threshold, and carries the mass_equivalent flag. The call rule's thresholds are unchanged.
    2. Flag coverage (option 2). N-terminal two-residue block reads such as AG→Q are treated as blocks and flagged mass_equivalent. Swap-type ALTs are flagged too.
    3. Swap support (option 3 not taken). Overlapping peptides that carry the same swap still count as distinct support. Swaps are flagged, not demoted.
    4. G1 scope. G1 (f1_min.variants_high 0.90, unchanged) scores unflagged high calls against identifiable truth: truth variants that are mass-equivalent to the reference leave the recall denominator too. Flagged calls are reported, not scored. Before this applies, the statistician recomputes G1 under this scope (before and after the flag changes) and records it here; the tolerance is never widened (R3).
    5. Attacks. A6a: every isobaric non-swap ALT is flagged mass_equivalent. A6b: every random adjacent swap call is flagged (replaces "no high call"). A6c: unchanged (expected fail).
    6. Rows outside substitutions (block, swap, frameshift high rows): out of G1 (M7-13 Q2); their precision is recorded per type. Open: whether they get their own gate (backlog).

    - Identifiable-truth rule: a truth row leaves the recall denominator when
    giap.variants.flags.mass_equivalent(ref_aa, alt_aa, variants.mass_equivalent_tol_da) is true
    (the same test, including the B.5 modification deltas, that sets the call flag). It removes 26 %
    of recall-eligible truth at 100k (800 → 593) and 24 % at 1M, mostly residue-plus-modification
    matches (V>L, N>K, D>E, S>T, G>A …), not plain isobaric residues.
    - 37 % of high substitution calls are flagged and reported but not scored.
    - Residual false positives are mostly unannotated start-codon M calls (ALT M at the peptide
    N-terminus over ref L) and reversed-stretch decompositions; residual false negatives are mostly
    candidate-tier rows (call threshold, not flags).
    ## Implementation rules (from the evidence)
    - A6a: flag a substitution when an ALT peptide also aligns as the 2→1 block
    ref[p-1:p+1] → alt with its prefix shifted by one (q = 0, or P[:q] == ref[p-1-q:p-1]).
    This fixes 24 of 24 unflagged isobaric rows. No right-hand or looser form (they flag true
    positives by chance).
    - A6b: flags_for (src/giap/variants/flags.py) flags every swap row mass_equivalent
    (75 of 75).

    "Identifiable" (Khoa, 2026-10-23): it uses the modification-inclusive mass_equivalent test above, not a strictly isobaric-residue rule. The report states the share of truth this removes (26 % at 100k, 24 % at 1M).
