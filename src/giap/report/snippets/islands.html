Islands are runs of adjacent transcription units whose detrended abundance scores are jointly high or low along a replicon. Each window sum is compared with the maximum of a TU-block bootstrap of the same scores, taken over every scanned replicon, so one family-wise error rate covers the whole run; a window is reported when its p-value is at most stats.alpha.context.

The FWER is claimed for a dependence regime, not for a replicon size. It is not claimed where strong TU-level autocorrelation is resolved by Moran's I only to a short lag (block length 7 or less). No per-replicon statistic certifies the domain, so nothing is flagged; instead the report lists, per replicon:

  • the estimated correlation length (l-hat), or why it was not estimated;
  • the bootstrap block length used (block_len);
  • the lag-1 Moran's I of the detrended TU scores.
