The UniOP preflight probe and the R fgsea engine no longer pull container images: their `docker run` carries `--pull=never`. A missing fgsea image fails at once with a message to run `gippa setup` (detected from docker's "No such image" and podman's "image not known" errors), and under docker preflight's fgsea row checks the pinned image is present locally. Nextflow's own step images are not covered yet (N6).
