`gippa setup` with a `--db-dir` that cannot be created, or an existing one it cannot write to, now stops with one line (`cannot create --db-dir <path>: <reason>` or `cannot write to <path>: <reason>`) and exit 1 before printing the plan, instead of an internal error; a full disk while creating it is exit 2. The free-space line names the existing directory it measured ([#503](https://github.com/khoa-yelo/gippa/issues/503)). A failed `apptainer pull` or `docker pull` is retried once, its message keeps the last stderr lines, and setup ends with a line naming the images that were not pulled and saying to rerun `gippa setup`; images that hit a full disk or a digest mismatch point to their messages above instead ([#504](https://github.com/khoa-yelo/gippa/issues/504)).
