N-terminal peptides of genes annotated with a GTG or TTG start codon (Bakta writes their first residue as `M`) now map exactly to their own gene, flagged `initiator_m` in `peptide_hits.tsv` (output schema 1.2), instead of being lost, mapped as a strain difference with an ALT `M` at the start codon, or placed only at a chance copy elsewhere. The six-frame index is now format 4 and is rebuilt automatically on the next run; an older index still loads but gives no initiator credit and logs a warning to rebuild it.
